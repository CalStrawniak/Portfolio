About

Hey there! I’m Cal, a 2023 BFA Film graduate from the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee. I’m passionate about visual storytelling and creating engaging content that connects with audiences.

My hands-on experience includes photography at Jostens and producing video content for social media and marketing campaigns at Weyco Group. I love diving into any creative challenge, from quick social clips to immersive visual experiences.

Through customer-facing roles, I discovered how much satisfaction I get from helping others. Video editing lets me bring that same mindset into a creative space, helping brands tell their stories clearly while connecting with their audience.
